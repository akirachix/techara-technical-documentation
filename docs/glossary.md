# Glossary

Definitions of technical, operational and energy-sector terms used throughout the GridFit documentation.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| CT clamp | Current transformer clamp. A sensor that measures electrical current flowing through a conductor without requiring a direct electrical connection to the conductor. |
| Critical load | Equipment or electrical demand that must remain powered for a business to carry out essential operations. |
| Duty cycle | The proportion of a specified period during which equipment is operating, usually expressed as a percentage. |
| ERB | Energy Regulation Board. Zambia's energy-sector regulator, responsible for regulating energy undertakings and services. |
| Field operator | A person who uses the field application to register businesses, record equipment and carry out metering-related activities. |
| Fit check | GridFit's rules-based assessment of whether a business's electricity requirements can be supported by a mini-grid's available capacity and operating conditions. |
| Flexible load | Electrical demand that can be shifted, reduced or scheduled to accommodate available electricity supply. |
| kW vs kWh | Kilowatt (kW) measures the rate of electrical power at a given moment. Kilowatt-hour (kWh) measures the amount of electrical energy consumed or generated over time. |
| Load shedding | The deliberate temporary interruption of electricity supply to some consumers to manage insufficient generation or network capacity. |
| Mini-grid | A local electricity network that supplies multiple consumers from one or more generation sources and operates independently of, or separately from, the main electricity grid. |
| Mini-grid operator | An organisation or entity responsible for operating and managing a mini-grid and its electricity services. |
| PUE | Power Usage Effectiveness. A data-centre energy-efficiency metric calculated as total facility energy divided by IT equipment energy. |
| PWA | Progressive Web App. A web application designed to provide an app-like experience through supported browsers and devices. |
| PZEM-004T | An electrical measurement module used to measure parameters such as voltage, current, active power and energy consumption. |
| SME | Small and Medium-sized Enterprise. A business classified as small or medium-sized under the applicable national or institutional definition. |
| SoC | State of Charge. The estimated amount of energy remaining in a battery, usually expressed as a percentage of its usable capacity. |
| Spare capacity | The portion of a mini-grid's available generation or supply capacity that is not currently committed to existing demand and may be available for additional loads. |
| ZEDSI | Acronym retained from the original project glossary. Its full meaning has not yet been verified and requires confirmation from the project team. |

## Notes

- GridFit's fit check is a decision-support assessment, not an engineering approval or regulatory certification.
- The meaning of spare capacity depends on the assessment period, existing demand, generation availability and system constraints.
- Hardware references describe intended metering components; they do not by themselves establish that a device has been installed or validated.
